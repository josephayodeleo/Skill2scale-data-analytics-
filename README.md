This repository presents my work from the Skill2Scale Four-Week Virtual Internship Project in Data Analytics.
The project demonstrates an end-to-end analytics workflow, beginning with data auditing and cleaning and progressing through visualization, dashboard development, predictive analysis, executive reporting, and business recommendations.
The project was completed for the fictional client Tech Savvy Solutions, a digital marketing consultancy serving e-commerce brands.

Data disclaimer: The source datasets referenced in the project brief were not attached. Numerical results in this portfolio are therefore based on a simulated dataset created for demonstration. The figures should be replaced with verified client data before being presented as actual business results.

Project ObjectivesThe main objectives of this project were to:
•Establish a reliable structure for storing and documenting data.
•Clean and validate campaign-performance data.
•Define meaningful marketing and business KPIs.
•Build dashboards and visualizations for decision-making.
•Identify performance trends and campaign segments.
•Produce a directional revenue projection.
•Translate analytical findings into practical recommendations.
•Communicate results through professional reports and presentations.

Weekly Project Work

Week 1 — Data Foundations and OrganizationThe first week focused on preparing the data for reliable analysis. I audited the raw campaign extract, removed one duplicate record, resolved one missing revenue value using a documented channel-month median rule, standardized an inconsistent campaign name, and produced a cleaned dataset.Deliverables included:•Data audit and cleaning report.•Cleaned campaign dataset.•Data dictionary.•Folder and file-management SOP.•Summary statistics table.•Professional weekly data-insights email.View Week 1 folder

Week 2 — Visualization and Dashboard DevelopmentThe second week focused on converting cleaned data into clear business visuals. I developed a marketing-performance dashboard showing key performance indicators, top campaigns by ROI, monthly revenue trends, and channel performance.The five primary KPIs were:1.Blended ROI.2.Click-through rate.3.Conversion rate.4.Cost per lead.5.Month-over-month revenue growth.The analysis indicated that Email delivered the strongest combination of ROI and lead efficiency.View Week 2 folder

Week 3 — Business Intelligence and Predictive InsightsThe third week focused on deeper analysis. I reviewed revenue trends, assessed the relationship between ad spend and leads, segmented campaigns according to ROI, developed a directional month-four revenue projection, and prepared a simulated competitor comparison.The spend-leads correlation was 0.07, indicating a weak positive association in the small sample. The directional projection was $559,000 for month four, although the estimate should be validated with a longer historical dataset.View Week 3 folder

Week 4 — Executive Reporting and Portfolio PresentationThe fourth week focused on communicating the results to decision-makers. I prepared an end-of-month analytics report, developed a business optimization proposal, created an executive dashboard, produced a portfolio presentation, and documented my professional reflection.The primary recommendation was to implement a centralized refreshable marketing-performance pipeline connecting controlled raw-data storage, documented cleaning rules, a single reporting table, and a dashboard refresh process.View Week 4 folder

Main Business Insights
Email was the most efficient channel
Email produced the highest blended ROI at 688.9% and the lowest channel cost per lead at $27.75. High-performing programs included VIP Early Access and Loyalty Win-Back campaigns.

Revenue increased each month
Revenue increased from $389,000 in July to $447,000 in August and $502,000 in September. September was the strongest month by revenue and conversions.

Scaling should be disciplined
The weak spend-leads correlation indicates that increasing spend alone may not generate proportional lead growth. Budget decisions should also consider campaign quality, audience targeting, creative performance, and conversion efficiency.Reporting automation would improve decision-making
A centralized and refreshable reporting pipeline would reduce manual preparation, improve data traceability, and help stakeholders access consistent KPIs more quickly.
